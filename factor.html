What is Factor Analysis?

X = ΛF + ε

Factor analysis attempts to explain correlations among observed variables using a smaller number of underlying latent factors. Variables that are strongly related may form a common factor.

Common steps:

1. Examine the correlation matrix
2. Check KMO and Bartlett's test
3. Extract factors
4. Examine eigenvalues and scree plot
5. Decide the number of factors
6. Examine communalities
7. Rotate factors for easier interpretation
8. Name and interpret the factors

General Classroom Guide

KMO:
≥ 0.90 excellent
0.80–0.89 very good
0.70–0.79 good
0.60–0.69 acceptable
0.50–0.59 poor
< 0.50 generally unsuitable

Bartlett's test: a small p-value suggests that the correlation matrix differs from an identity matrix, supporting factor analysis.
