Official Announcement

ICETASD 2027 is coming soon

The Nepal Statistical Society is pleased to announce the upcoming International Conference on Emerging Trends and Applications in Statistics and Data Science – 2027 (ICETASD 2027). Detailed information on the conference dates, venue, call for papers, registration and programme will be announced soon.

About ICETASD 2027

Connecting research, statistics and data-driven innovation

ICETASD 2027 will provide an international forum for sharing recent research, methodologies, applications and practical experiences in Statistics and Data Science.

The conference will bring together researchers, academicians, statisticians, data scientists, professionals, policymakers and students from Nepal and abroad.

The programme will emphasize emerging statistical methods, data-driven approaches, interdisciplinary research and real-world applications.

Conference Themes

These broad areas indicate the scope of ICETASD 2027. Additional tracks may be announced with the call for papers.

01

Statistics & Data Science

Modern statistics, data science, AI and machine learning.

02

03

06

07

Governance & Policy

08

Emerging applications of quantitative methods across disciplines.
