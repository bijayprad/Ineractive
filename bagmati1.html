Workshop Purpose

From research idea to publication

This workshop is designed to develop practical knowledge and skills required to plan, design, conduct, and communicate academic research systematically. Participants work through key stages of the research process, including research problem formulation, literature review, methodological planning, sampling, data collection, analysis, interpretation, proposal development, and publication.
