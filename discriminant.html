How to Interpret

Wilks' Lambda: values closer to 0 indicate stronger separation between groups; values closer to 1 indicate weaker separation.

Canonical discriminant function: forms a weighted combination of predictors that separates the groups.

Classification accuracy: percentage of observations correctly classified by the fitted discriminant rule.

Confusion matrix: rows are actual groups and columns are predicted groups.
